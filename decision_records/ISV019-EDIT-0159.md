# ISV019-EDIT-0159 — complete TeX-group foreign-identity protection

Date: 2026-08-20 (Europe/Berlin)

## Decision

Protect fifteen complete, already-delimited identity, venue, and signature
groups with `\foreign{...}`. The groups occur inside existing `\mbox`,
`\emph`, or `\textsc` commands. No word, spelling, punctuation,
mathematical expression, source locator, or producer text is changed.

This is a project-internal script-boundary decision. It prevents the
deterministic Cyrillic projection from converting foreign identity surfaces;
it is not a claim that the protected strings are Interslavic vocabulary, nor
native-speaker or community certification.

## Exact scope

The twelve changed physical lines contain fifteen complete groups and
seventeen scanner-visible blockers:

- `\mbox{E. Müller}`, two complete `\mbox{Müller}` groups;
- three complete `\emph{Weber}` groups;
- `\mbox{Göttingen}`;
- two complete `Nachr. v. d. Ges. d. Wiss. zu Göttingen ...` venue groups;
- `\emph{Du Pasquier}`, `\emph{Loewy}`, and
  `\emph{Poincaré}`;
- two complete `\mbox{A.~Ostrowski}` groups; and
- the complete signature `\textsc{Emmy Noether, Göttingen}`.

Each becomes, for example, `\mbox{\foreign{E. Müller}}` or
`\emph{\foreign{Weber}}`. The wrapper is inside the existing
typographical command, so typography stays where it was. No wrapper is nested
inside a pre-existing `\foreign` island.

## Why this tranche is safe

The current-head frontier index reconciles all 307 remaining projection
blockers exactly with the earlier exhaustive foreign/title/venue/citation
role evidence. The index has 307 fresh occurrence rows on 172 lines and a
multiplicity-sensitive zero-difference join. This tranche deliberately selects
only the unambiguous complete TeX groups from that larger inventory.

None of the twelve lines belongs to one of the six active local producer
mirrors. Therefore the cumulative base is the only source target and no
producer body or cumulative `% Identity` receipt changes.

The transform was independently reconstructed without importing the
applicator. That reconstruction rediscovered the same fifteen groups, produced
the same successor bytes, and reduced the current v4 frontier from 307 to 290.
