# ISV019-EDIT-0133 — Paper 2 five-column schema and rosette restoration

Status: applied, deterministically built, visually checked, and independently replayed on 2026-08-14. This decision affects only the unpublished Interslavic v019 working successor; it has no publication or DOI effect.

## Decision

Restore the Paper 2 continuation schema from the inherited four-column truncation to the five columns carried by active German authority ED0014. Restore the omitted terminal upper row, place the same verified source rosette at both continuation joins, and make the lower `*)` source-note relationship explicit.

The surrounding Interslavic wording and the 148-byte localized note remain byte-for-byte unchanged. No formula variable, citation, label, cross-reference, or unrelated unit was edited.

## Why this was necessary

The missing fifth column, terminal row, and two attachment marks materially obscured how the lower fragment continues the upper schema. ED0014 lines 606–658, the printed/GDZ witnesses, and the retained exact-5000 crops agree on the five-column structure and the two matching rosettes.

The embedded mark is the adjudicated 108×108 RGBA authority asset. Both uses retain the accepted 14.726716981132075 pt frame width, 9.44444 pt advance, −3.85 pt raise, 530 ppi placement, and disabled interpolation.

## Producer lineage

The new standalone Section 01 producer uses only the pinned historical producer's preamble shell. Its body was reconstructed from the current normalized Unit003 body in the live cumulative source, then given the bounded display repair. It was not rebuilt from the obsolete historical body: the live pre-edit body is 6,562 bytes, while the historical body is 6,455 bytes and differs across the already completed Interslavic normalization history.

This distinction is important. Reusing the historical body would silently undo substantive language work even if the diagram itself looked correct.

## Alternatives rejected

- Retain four columns: this preserves a source-layout omission, not a coherent variant.
- Use a generic star: the source carries two matching non-generic rosette marks.
- Redraw or smooth the glyph: the accepted source-derived bitmap already has pinned custody and placement receipts.
- Reword the localized note: it is adequate and exact preservation avoids gratuitous translation drift.
- Reconstruct the producer from its old body: that would regress the normalized Interslavic text.
- Modify frozen v047, public r35, or the online standalone record: this repair belongs only to v019.
