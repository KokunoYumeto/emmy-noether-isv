## Classification and decision

This is an append-only correction of an original EDIT0014 coverage defect and
the corresponding current-source residue. It is not a new lexical choice and
not a claim that later editing reintroduced the forms.

EDIT0014 selected the official `sostojati se iz` family, but its census and
residual matcher covered only spellings beginning `sostoj-`. Fourteen
same-sense `sostaj-` forms consequently survived both the exact EDIT0014
predecessor and a deterministic replay of EDIT0014’s output. EDIT0142 replaces
the same 14 surviving forms in the current cumulative base:

- twelve `sostaji` forms become context-appropriate `sostoji se` or `sostoji`;
- `sostaječu` becomes `sostoječu se`;
- `sostajuča` becomes `sostoječa se`.

The sites occupy exactly 13 lines: 623, 677, 914, 2392, 3744, 3781 (two
sites), 4383, 5984, 6021, 6030, 7168, 11162, and 21961. The cumulative
canonical-family count changes from 145 to 159 and the live `sostaj-` residue
changes from 14 to zero.

The output base is 1,899,091 bytes, SHA-256
`E5C333BAC9283EC1A3850F4CF9DD226593C0606D2F68930B2CBD0DAF0897F66A`.
Exact positional reversal of only the 14 EDIT0142 successors restores the
sealed EDIT0141 base, 1,899,052 bytes, SHA-256
`FE1A197641913CE20CF3925EBEC6850178A23BD0C7EA09A4C764896E553F55C9`.
