## Decision

Replace the 19 live, standalone `Zbog`/`zbog` tokens in the assembled Latin
base with case-preserving `Zaradi`/`zaradi`. The 19 sites occupy exactly 17
source lines: 4582, 9919, 9966, 10025, 11115, 13467, 13540, 15998, 16909,

`FE1A197641913CE20CF3925EBEC6850178A23BD0C7EA09A4C764896E553F55C9`.
An exact positional reverse of only those 19 successor tokens restores the

## Reasons and evidence

The official dictionary snapshot has one exact headword `zaradi`, classified
as a preposition with `(+2)`, and no `zbog` headword. Its glosses include “for
the sake of” and “on account of”; its intelligibility row is broad across the
Slavic comparison set. The official grammar says derived prepositions
generally govern the genitive. A pinned `bez (+2)` row supplies the local
crosswalk from dictionary case code `(+2)` to the grammar’s genitive section.
The grammar’s finite preposition list does not itself name `zaradi`; that
omission is retained as adverse evidence rather than hidden.

Every target was independently aligned to German ED0015. Its local connective
is causal or logical (`wegen`, `Wegen`, or `Infolge`) rather than purposive,
and each successor occurs with an already compatible genitive phrase or a
formula. Eleven sites are classified as genitive constructions and eight as
formula-governed constructions. This makes the change a controlled house-
register normalization, not a blind replacement or a native-language vote.

The dictionary row itself keeps Croatian `zbog` and Serbian `због` among its
translation-column equivalents. Twelve pinned historical origins retain 19
legacy `zbog` sites in total. Those bytes are useful lineage and intelligibility
evidence; they are deliberately not rewritten and are not active translation
authority for the cumulative base.
