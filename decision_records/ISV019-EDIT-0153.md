# ISV019-EDIT-0153 — Zurich city adjective

## Decision

At `source_latin/base-papers1-43-isv.tex`, physical line 24190, replace the
single visible-prose surface `zürihsko` with `curišsko`:

```text
moje zürihsko predavanje
→
moje curišsko predavanje
```

This is a one-locus contextual normalization. It closes the only explicit
lexical hold recorded by sealed `ISV019-EDIT-0152`. It does not establish a
general official Interslavic headword for Zurich, certify native acceptance,
or prove that the Latin edition is linguistically complete.

## Why this reading

The project-active German authority, ED0015, says `meinen Züricher Vortrag` at
logical line 19798. The referent is therefore the city of Zurich and the word
is an adjective agreeing with “lecture”; it is not a reference to Switzerland
as a country.

The pinned official Interslavic orthography states that its etymological
alphabet does not include special borrowing letters such as German or French
`ü`. Retaining `zürihsko` would therefore leave a visibly non-Interslavic
letter in reader prose and would keep the active Cyrillic scanner blocked at
this locus.

The pinned official dictionary contains no Zurich adjective. It does,
however, provide the directly relevant project-house morphological analogy
`Čeh` → `češsky`, with the example surface `Češsko`. This supports the
`h` → `š` alternation before `-sk-` and a neuter `-sko` form. The registered
Russian and Ukrainian readers independently use city adjectives with initial
/ts/: `цюрихский доклад` and `цюрихську доповідь`. Taken together, these
sources support `curišsko` as a transparent pan-Slavic project-house
compromise.

Confidence is 0.80: strong enough for this edition’s contextual decision, but
explicitly below official-dictionary or community-validation status.

## Rejected alternatives

- `zürihsko`: retains non-Interslavic `ü` and remains unprojectable.
- `zurihsko`: leaves both German `z` and the unadapted `h+sk` sequence.
- `curihsko`: supplies initial `c` but omits the supported `h` → `š`
  alternation.
- `cjurihsko`: overfits an East-Slavic spelling and is less branch-neutral.
- `švejcarsko`: changes the referent from the city of Zurich to Switzerland.
