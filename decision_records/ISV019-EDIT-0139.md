## Decision

Replace the complete live `shvat-` family according to sentence sense rather than by one global spelling substitution. Seven grouping senses become forms of official `grupovati`; six abstract-conception senses become forms of official `pojmati`:

- grouping: `shvatet` → `grupujut`; `shvaćanje` → `grupovanje`; `shvaćajut` → `grupujut`; two `Shvatimo` → `Grupujemo`; `shvačene` → `grupovane`; one `shvatiti` → `grupovati`;
- abstract conception: three `shvatiti` → `pojmati`; `shvati` → `pojmaje`; `shvačeno` → `pojmano`; `shvata` → `pojmaje`.

The source sites occur in Papers 19 (6), 20 (1), 22 (1), 24 (3), 27 (1), and 28 (1). Paper 22 and active Paper 24 have synchronized local producers, so their four producer twins and the two assembled identity receipts were updated in the same source transaction. The inactive Paper 24 reconstruction remains byte-identical and retains three historical tokens as lineage evidence.

## Evidence and authority

The official dictionary distinguishes the semantic domains. Physical row 14624 defines `shvatiti` as the perfective transitive “grab, grasp, seize”; row 3715 defines imperfective transitive `grupovati` as “group” and supplies the live form `grupujut`; row 11463 defines imperfective transitive `pojmati` as “understand, conceive, comprehend, perceive, grasp”. The official verb grammar supplies the conjugation and derived-form framework.

All 13 aligned active German ED0015 sites support the split. The seven grouping sites use `zusammenfassen`, `Zusammenfassung`, or equivalent explicit grouping language; the six conception sites use `fassen`, `auffassen`, or `aufgefaßt` in abstract interpretive senses. German ED0015 is project-active semantic and source control, not native-language, canonical, critical-edition, or translation certification. The categorized corpus remains comparator evidence only and is not promoted to native review or terminology approval.
