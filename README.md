This is the complete v047 Interslavic edition of the corpus defined by German authority `NOETH-DE-ED-0011`: Papers 1–43, Work 44, Paper 45, and the bibliography. The linked Latin-script reader is the easiest way to begin. The release also includes editable Latin- and Cyrillic-script sources and a deterministic Cyrillic reader in the supporting archive.
