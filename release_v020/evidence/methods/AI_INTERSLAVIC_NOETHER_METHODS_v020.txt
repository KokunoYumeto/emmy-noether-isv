                                                         Abstract
      Medžuslovjansky. Tuta statja dokumentuje tvorjenje polnogo medžuslovjanskogo izdanja matem-
      atičnyh děl Emmy Noether v redakcijnoj latinici i deterministično izvedenoj kirilici. Cělj jest praktično
      čitanje govoriteljami slovjanskyh jezykov, uključajuči čitateljev bez anglijskogo jezyka, a ne sama
      demonstracija jezyčnogo eksperimenta. Izdanje v020 ne prěpisuje latiničny kanon: ono popravjaje
      role, ktore upravljajut izvedenjem kirilice. Bylo pregledano vseh 4 560 naslědženyh zapisov rolij po
      pojavjenju; popravljeno bylo 364 klasifikacij i dodano 8 nedostajučih granic polnyh neizměnjenyh
      identitetov. Projektor v7 dokazano izvršaje vseh 372 efektov: 346 ohranjajut latinny tekst, a 26
      projicirajut go v kirilicu. Skutočna izhodna razlika sodrži 125 linij vnutri licencovanogo množstva od
      309 linij i nulu izměn izvan njega. Dva polna čitateljnika imajut 565 i 588 stranic; relizna kontrola
      imaje 38 uspěšnyh prověrok i nulu pogrěšek. “Nula nejasnostij” znači samo, že v manifestu ne ostalo
      nerěšenyh klasifikacij rolij. Ona ne znači rodno-govoriteljsku, obćinsku ili naučnu certifikaciju. Vse
      iztočniki, rěšenja, programy, kontrolne sumy, ograničenja i javne adresy sut ostavljene tako, da druga
      sesija ili recenzent može povtoriti, kritikovati ili monotono prodolžiti izdanje.
      English. This paper documents the production of a complete Interslavic edition of Emmy Noether’s
      mathematical works in editorial Latin script and deterministically derived Cyrillic. The purpose is prac-
      tical reading by speakers of Slavic languages, including readers without English, rather than a display
      of linguistic experimentation. Release v020 does not rewrite the Latin canon; it corrects the roles that
      govern Cyrillic derivation. All 4,560 inherited role records were reviewed occurrence by occurrence;
      364 classifications were corrected and 8 missing boundaries for complete uninflected identities were
      added. Projector v7 demonstrably realizes all 372 effects: 346 preserve Latin material and 26 project
      it into Cyrillic. The actual output delta comprises 125 lines within a licensed set of 309 lines and zero
      changes outside it. The two complete readers contain 565 and 588 pages; release QA reports 38 pass-
      ing checks and zero errors. “Zero ambiguity” means only that the role manifest contains no unresolved
      classifications. It is not native-speaker, community, or scholarly certification. Sources, decisions, soft-
      ware, hashes, limitations, and public access points are retained so that another session or reviewer can
      reproduce, challenge, or monotonically extend the edition.

3 Mašinno čitljivo odkryvanje kanona
Povtorimost začinaje s odkryvanjem. Buduća sesija ne smije ugadyvati ktory iz podobnyh fajlov jest kanon.
Zato koren repozitorija imaje CANON_INDEX.json, ktory deklaruje:
  • jezyk i BCP 47 označenja pisma;
  • četyri kanonične latinne fajly i četyri izvedene kirilične fajly;
  • točne bajtove i SHA-256 vsakej upravljajučej jedinice;
  • latinnu redakcijnu glavu i identitet žurnala, do ktorogo se može samo dodavati;
  • manifest rolij, projektor, test i prověrčny dokument;
  • německy avtoritet, granice pisanja i granice dozvoljenyh tvrdženj;
  • deterministične komandy sgrađanja i imena javnyh artefaktov.
   Indeks jest deklarativny, a ne samopotvrđajuči. On govori agentu čto objekty sut; izvršive prověrky
dokazujut, jest li deklarovana identiteta ješče pravdiva. Tuto razděljenje odklanjaje kružno pravilo “na-
jnověje izgledajuči fajl jest kanon” i dava osnovu, k ktoroj druga transkripcija, prěvod ili azbučna projekcija
može bezpečno pridati novy sloj.


4 Čto AI děla i čto ne može rěšiti samo
Projekt uporablja AI kako redakcijny instrument, ne kako iztočny avtoritet. Mašinny trud obimaje sravnjenje
větnyh kontekstov, tvorjenje kandidatnyh inventarov, klasifikaciju pojavjenj, formulovanje alternativ, gen-
erovanje strukturnyh testov i tvorjenje javnoj dokumentacije. Deterministične programy potom autentifikuju
vhody, priměnjajut samo zamrznute rěšenja i odklanjajut nepredvidženu izměnu.
   Metoda ima slědujuči cikl:
 1. zamrznuti iztočny i ciljevy bajtovy vektor;
 2. najdti materialne jezyčne ili rolne kandidaty bez avtomatičnogo globalnogo zaměnjenja;
 3. pregledati vsako pojavjenje v větnom, bibliografičnom, matematičnom i TeX-kontekstu;
 4. zapisati izbranu rolu, dokaz, odklonjene alternativy, neuvěrjenost i poslědstva;
 5. napraviti manifest ili redakcijno rěšenje, vezano k točnomu lokatoru i kontrolnoj sumě;
 6. izvesti izhod čistym programom i dokazati dopuščeny obseg razlike;
 7. sgraditi, iztegnuti tekst, prověriti fonty, navigaciju, strukturu i vizualne stranice;
 8. publikovati izvorniki, dokazy i haše zajedno s čitateljnikom, potom anonimno pročitati javne bajty nazad.
   AI-prědlog ne stavaje kanonom zato, že zvuči uvěrlivo. On stavaje dopustimym samo kogda jest lokali-
zovany, podporjen deklarovanym dokazom, ograničeny v obsegu i povtorimo prověrjen. Kde dokaz ne jest
dostatočny, pravilen rezultat jest ograničeno tvrdženje ili zapisano nerěšeno pytanje, a ne izmišljena certi-
fikacija.

7 Matematična i TeX-věrnost
Prirodnojezyčna normalizacija jest podredžena matematičnoj identitetě. Zaštićene granice obimajut matem-
atičny režim, imena komand, opcije komand, oznaky, odkazy, ključe citat, puti, komentare i dokazano
izvornojezyčne argumenty. Za vsaky latinni/kiriličny par odstranjenje Unicode-bukv i kombinovanyh
znakov dava tu samu ne-bukvenu struju. Taka prověrka ohranjaje zagrady, kontrolne slova, operatory, čisla,
poravnateljne znaky i matematične symbole.
   Ranějši neuspěh pokazuje, začto jezyčny skener ne dostaje. Projektor v5 je pogrěšno čital TeX-jedinicu
24em kako vidimo slovo i stvoril kiriličnu jedinicu, ktora ne jest zakonny TeX. Projektor v6 je zato dobil
eksplicitnu gramatiku numeričnyh razměrov, a v7 ju naslědžuje i ponovno prověrjaje. Ohranjeno jest 447
raněje izloženih pojavjenj: 219 em i 228 pt; kiriličny ostatok razměrne jedinice jest nula. Neuspěh ostavaje
v dokazah, ibo “čisty” leksičny rezultat ne dokazuje izvršivu pravilnost iztočnogo programa.

8 Determinističny izvod v7
V7 jest čista funkcija četyrěh zamrznutyh latinnyh fajlov, pregledanogo manifesta rolij i pinovanyh zavis-
nostij. On:
 1. autentifikuje razměr i SHA-256 vsakoj latiničnoj jedinice;

 3. razděljuje TeX na vidime i zaštićene role;
 4. sjedinjuje pregledane granice imen, citat, rimskyh označenj i razměrov;
 5. projiciraje ostale vidime riječi zamrznutym medžuslovjanskim transliteratorom;
 6. nezavisno rekonstruuje izhod s tragom i zahtěvaje bajtnu identitetu dvoh izvodov;
 7. dokazuje vsaky iz 372 efektov i vsaku izměnjenu liniju;
 8. piše izhody transakcijno samo poslě vseh prověrok.
    Hostilny testovi naměrno měnjajut iztočny bajt, izostavljajut jedinicu topologije, prědlagajut nebezpečny
cilj izhoda, kvarjajut pin manifesta i kvarjajut pin starogo izhoda. Vse pet slučajev bylo odklonjeno prě

Izdanje može s uvěrjenostju tvrdžiti točny vhodny vektor, povtorimu redakcijnu historiju, polny pregled

prověrjenu PDF-strukturu. Ono ne tvrdži:
  • rodno-govoriteljsku certifikaciju ili soglasje medžu slovjanskymi obćinami;
  • potvrđenje medžuslovjanskoj obćiny ili oficialnogo standarda;
  • nezavisnu naučnu recenziju;
  • novu kritičnu redakciju německyh originalov;
  • že računalna prověrka dokazuje idiomatičnost ili optimalny leksičny izbor;
  • že kirilica jest nezavisny dokaz pravilnosti prěvoda.
   Člověčsky vlastnik projekta je oprědělil obseg, kustodiju, dostup i publikacijne cěljy. Dostupny dokaz
ne opravdava tvrdženje, že člověk ili rodny govoritelj je liniju-po-liniji recenzoval cěly jezyčny korpus.

pomaganym trudom i prověrjenu determinističnymi kontrolami. Ona ne značet ljudsku jezyčnu certifikaciju.
   Ta ograničenja ne sut razlog skryti dělo. Javny izvorniky, lokatory i kontrolne sumy prětvarjajut neso-
glasje v izvršivu popravku: recenzent može imenovati město, prědložiti lučše čitanje, citovati protivny dokaz

13 Object, purpose, and readership
The object is one complete Interslavic language edition of a bounded Emmy Noether corpus: numbered
Papers 1–43, lecture work 44, Paper 45, and the bibliography. It is not an anthology of samples and not
a collection of unrelated transcriptions. The public reader provides two writing surfaces of one language
object:
  • isv‐Latn is the canonical editorial language text;
  • isv‐Cyrl is a deterministic projection of that text, not an independent translation witness.
   This distinction is methodologically important. A derived script cannot itself confirm the translation from
which it was created. Linguistic corrections enter only the Latin canon; Cyrillic must be regenerated, tested,
built, and visually reviewed after the input bytes are frozen. V020 is an especially clear case: the Latin text
remains byte-identical to v019, but the derived Cyrillic is corrected because the role review found wrong
boundaries among language text, names, quotations, and Roman labels.
   The practical heuristic for linguistic progress is not “how Slavic-looking is the prose?” It is the conjunction
of four questions:
 1. Can speakers of different Slavic languages plausibly parse the sentence without English?
 2. Is the selected form supported by the declared Interslavic lexicon or grammar, or by a clearly recorded
    project-house rule?
 3. Does the form preserve the exact mathematical referent, quantification, and argument relation of the
    German source?
 4. Is every exception, identity, quotation, or ambiguity represented by a reproducible role rather than silently
    omitted?
   The edition is therefore best understood as a corrigible scholarly working edition: public, inspectable,
hash-bound, and open to improvement, but not represented as native-speaker-certified or as a critical edition.


14 Authority and custody architecture
The method separates source authority, language canon, derived script, editorial evidence, and public prod-
ucts. No layer may silently assume the function of another.

16 What AI does and what it cannot decide alone
The project uses AI as an editorial instrument, not as source authority. Machine work includes sentence-level
comparison, candidate inventory construction, occurrence classification, formulation of alternatives, gener-
ation of structural tests, and production of public documentation. Deterministic programs then authenticate
inputs, apply only frozen decisions, and reject unintended changes.
   The method follows this cycle:
 1. freeze source and target byte vectors;
 2. locate material linguistic or role candidates without automatic global replacement;
 3. review every occurrence in sentence, bibliography, mathematics, and TeX context;
 4. record the chosen role, evidence, rejected alternatives, uncertainty, and consequences;
 5. produce a manifest or editorial decision bound to an exact locator and hash;
 6. derive output with a pure program and prove the allowed delta;
 7. build, extract text, inspect fonts, navigation, structure, and rendered pages;
 8. publish sources, evidence, and hashes with the reader, then anonymously read the public bytes back.
   An AI proposal does not become canonical because it sounds plausible. It becomes admissible only when
localized, supported by declared evidence, bounded in scope, and reproducibly verified. Where evidence is
insufficient, the proper output is a narrow claim or a recorded open question, not invented certification.

18 Append-only linguistic normalization of the Latin canon
The Latin head inherits 171 material decisions from v019. A decision is admissible only when it identifies
a predecessor, exact target locus, proposed successor, relevant authority, rejected alternatives, motivation,
uncertainty, and post-application validation. An applicator authenticates every input and rejects ambiguous
targets. The ledger is append-only: a failed or superseded approach remains readable with its successor
instead of disappearing.
   The main linguistic classes are:
  • False friends and semantic partitions. Resemblance to Russian, Ukrainian, Polish, Czech, or South
    Slavic forms is insufficient. Existence, essence, composition, containment, assumption, consequence,
    and correspondence are adjudicated by sentence and mathematical sense.
  • Morphology and sentence role. Agreement, oblique cases, genitive plurals, participles, verb endings,
    and ordinal forms are classified before correction. Global suffix replacement is rejected because formu-
    las, names, and quotations can imitate an ordinary morpheme.
  • Connectives and proof logic. Causal, inferential, conditional, equivalence, contrastive, and apodosis
    functions are compared with the relation between source clauses. Word frequency must not reverse
    implication or weaken a quantifier.
  • Names, titles, and quotations. A complete uninflected identity and an original-language bibliographic
    surface are preserved. An integrated or inflected form is projected when it functions as Interslavic mor-
    phology.
   V020 does not reopen those Latin decisions. It corrects only the machine interpretation of which portions
of the frozen text are visible Interslavic and which are protected identity or structure.


19 Mathematical and TeX fidelity
Natural-language normalization is subordinate to mathematical identity. Protected boundaries include math
mode, command names, command options, labels, references, citation keys, paths, comments, and demon-
strated original-language arguments. For every Latin/Cyrillic pair, removing Unicode letters and combining
marks yields the same non-letter stream. This check preserves braces, control words, operators, numbers,
alignment marks, and mathematical symbols.

20 Deterministic v7 derivation
V7 is a pure function of the four frozen Latin files, the reviewed role manifest, and pinned dependencies. It:
 1. authenticates every Latin unit by byte count and SHA-256;
 2. verifies exact source and manifest topology;
 3. parses TeX into visible and protected roles;
 4. merges reviewed boundaries for names, quotations, Roman labels, and dimensions;
 5. projects every remaining visible word with the frozen Interslavic transliterator;
 6. independently reconstructs the output with a trace and requires byte identity between both derivations;
 7. proves every one of the 372 effects and every changed line;
 8. writes output transactionally only after every gate passes.
   Hostile tests deliberately mutate a source byte, omit a topology member, propose an unsafe output target,
corrupt the manifest pin, and corrupt an old-output pin. All five cases are rejected before writing. Two inde-
pendent calculations return byte-identical sets of four Cyrillic outputs. The test and projector are themselves
hashed, so “the same procedure” means identical code, not merely a similar description.

23 Limitations, human review, and responsible claims
The edition can make strong claims about an exact input vector, replayable editorial history, complete
projector-role review, deterministic Cyrillic derivation, bounded output delta, clean builds, and verified PDF
structure. It does not claim:
  • native-speaker certification or consensus among Slavic communities;
  • endorsement by the Interslavic community or an official standard;
  • independent scholarly peer review;
  • a new critical edition of the German originals;
  • that computational validation proves idiomaticity or an optimal lexical choice;
  • that Cyrillic is independent evidence for the translation.
   A human project owner set scope, custody, access, and publication goals. The available evidence does not
justify claiming that a human or native speaker reviewed the entire language corpus line by line. “Complete
review” in v020 therefore denotes completed occurrence-level role classification performed with machine
assistance and verified by deterministic controls. It does not denote human linguistic certification.
   These limitations are not reasons to hide the work. Public sources, locators, and hashes turn disagreement
into an executable correction: a reviewer can identify a locus, propose a better reading, cite contrary evidence,
and produce a monotonic successor without erasing the prior state.


24 Reusable protocol for other languages and transcriptions
The minimum transferable protocol is:
 1. define one source authority and one target-language canon;
 2. assign an explicit role to every derived script, transcription, or format;
 3. freeze source units with byte counts and hashes before editing;
 4. record material decisions append-only with rejected alternatives;
 5. separate visible language from protected syntax, mathematics, and identities;
 6. review candidates occurrence by occurrence and bind classifications to stable locators;
 7. validate meaning, language, mathematics, syntax, builds, extraction, fonts, and rendering as distinct
    gates;
 8. publish a human reader, editable sources, evidence, and a top-level hash manifest;
 9. expose a machine-readable canon index and deterministic entry points;
10. disclose absent review and remaining uncertainty;
11. issue immutable versions and anonymously verify public bytes.
   The central lesson is simple: an AI-produced language edition becomes scholarly usable when its choices
are not trapped in a model conversation. The canon, roles, evidence, failures, build commands, and public
identities must survive independently of the session that created them.
